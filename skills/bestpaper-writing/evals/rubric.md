# Blind-comparison rubric (Abstract + Introduction)

Candidates per case: the outputs of each configuration **and the original award paper’s abstract + introduction**, all anonymized as A, B, C, D (method names normalized). The judge scores each candidate 1–5 on every dimension, then ranks them.

| # | Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Gap and tension** | “existing methods have limitations” | concrete failure or missing capability | concrete gap with its reason, set up as a tension the idea resolves |
| D2 | **Core idea / handle** | method only described | idea stated but forgettable | one memorable sentence or term the reader would repeat |
| D3 | **Gap ↔ contribution** | unrelated | mostly matched | each gap answered by exactly one contribution |
| D4 | **Confident, accurate claims** | over-claims (unsupported SOTA/first/eliminates) **or** hedges and disclaimers that undercut the work | supported but timid or partly inflated | strongest supported claims, with numbers and scope; no limitations in abstract/intro |
| D5 | **Concreteness** | adjectives and abstractions | some numbers | examples, mechanisms and numbers carry the argument |
| D6 | **Positioning** | no prior work named | named, vague difference | closest work named with a specific, checkable difference |
| D7 | **Flow and economy** | repetitive, paragraphs restate themselves | readable | each sentence adds something; short sentences at pivots |
| D8 | **Best-paper feel** (holistic) | generic AI draft | solid main-conference paper | indistinguishable from an award paper |

Report per case: the score table, the ranking, and for each generated candidate the single most important difference from the original paper.
Penalize any invented number, dataset, baseline or citation by setting D4 to 1. Do not reward length.
